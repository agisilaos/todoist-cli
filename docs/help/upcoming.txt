Usage:
  todoist upcoming [days] [--project <id|name>] [--label <name>] [--sort due|priority] [--wide] [--ids-only]

Notes:
  - Shows tasks due from today through the next N days (default 7).
  - Includes tasks with due dates/datetimes only (tasks without due are excluded).

Examples:
  todoist upcoming
  todoist upcoming 14 --project Learning
  todoist upcoming --label reading --sort priority
