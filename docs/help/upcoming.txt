Usage:
  todoist upcoming [days] [--project <id|name>] [--label <name>] [--sort due|priority] [--wide] [--ids-only]

Notes:
  - Shows N days including today (default 7: today and the next 6 days, UTC).
  - Excludes overdue tasks and tasks without a due date.
  - Human output shows the UTC window, scope, coverage, and three-line titles.
  - Overview P1 is highest; --wide retains the detailed table (API priorities: 4 highest).
  - Use task view id:<id> for full text and secondary details.

Examples:
  todoist upcoming
  todoist upcoming 14 --project Learning
  todoist upcoming --label reading --sort priority
